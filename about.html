Our Origin & History

Founded in 2023 in Pietermaritzburg, KZN Collectibles & TCG was built by passionate tabletop enthusiasts who noticed a lack of dedicated gaming spaces in the Midlands. We started as an online trade group and expanded into a full retail hobby store and tournament center.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, authentic trading card products, fair market card valuations, and an inclusive, safe environment for both casual collectors and competitive tournament players.

Our Vision

To establish KwaZulu-Natal as a major competitive hub for national card gaming circuits and tabletop hobbyists.

Community Code of Conduct

  • Respect all players regardless of experience level or background.
  • Practice good hygiene and keep gaming tables clean after each match.
  • Use protective playmats and card sleeves during tournament matches.
  • Counterfeit or proxy cards are strictly prohibited in sanctioned events.
